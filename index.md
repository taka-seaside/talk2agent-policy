# Privacy Policy — Talk 2 Agent

Last updated: November 7, 2026
Developer: taka_seaside

Talk 2 Agent does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. The app has no server of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Built-in mode

Your speech is transcribed by the Pebble platform's dictation service. The resulting text is passed only to the agent plugin you selected in the app settings (such as Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok Bot). How that service handles the text is governed by its own privacy policy.

## External mode

Audio recordings are sent only to the endpoint URL you configure in the app settings. The developer cannot see or control the service at that URL.

## Settings

Your settings (URLs, headers, destination choices) are stored only on your phone and are never sent to the developer.

## Contact

Through the "Contact Developer" option on the Pebble Appstore listing page for Talk 2 Agent.
